## Character artwork

The three character GIFs are bundled unchanged.
The PNG fallbacks were extracted from the UI design reference images. They
include Claude-themed character artwork. No ownership of that
third-party artwork is claimed by this project. The original references are
retained under `docs/references` for design fidelity.

The application accepts replacement artwork through its assets directory.
The app's orange tray mark was created for this project.
